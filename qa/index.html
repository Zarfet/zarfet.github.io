01 · PROFESSIONAL WORK · ANONYMISED

Cypress migration testing & visual validation

A reusable quality workflow for a web-platform migration: validating routes, redirects, localised content, and visual consistency across key page areas.

The challenge

Migration work creates many ways for a familiar experience to break: an inaccessible route, a failed redirect, missing localised content, or a visual regression that only appears on a specific viewport.

The approach

Built Cypress checks for page loads, redirects, and localised content. Added screenshot comparison for headers, navigation, footers, page layouts, and dynamic content at desktop and mobile viewports.

What made it reusable

Clear test grouping, baseline and comparison workflows, and visual HTML reports gave the team a repeatable way to investigate regressions before release.

TESTING APPROACH

Good automation starts with good QA judgement.

  1. 01

    Understand the product and the risk

    Start with user flows, business priorities, integration points, and the areas most likely to damage a release.

  2. 02

    Choose the right level of testing

    Not every check belongs in end-to-end automation. The right balance protects speed, confidence, and maintainability.

  3. 03

    Make the work usable by the team

    Clear naming, shared fixtures, test plans, reports, and CI workflows turn testing into a team capability rather than one person’s knowledge.
